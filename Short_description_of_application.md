# ListManager - Smart Inventory Management

**ListManager** is an offline-first mobile app that revolutionizes inventory management through voice recognition and intelligent automation.

## What It Does

Walk through your warehouse, speak product names, and let ListManager handle the rest. The app automatically:

- Recognizes products from voice input
- Groups items by distributor
- Generates professional PDF order lists
- Syncs across all your devices in real-time

## Key Benefits

✅ **Hands-free operation** - Use voice commands while working  
✅ **Works 100% offline** - No internet required  
✅ **Real-time collaboration** - Multiple users, instant updates  
✅ **Smart matching** - Finds products even with variations in pronunciation  
✅ **Automatic organization** - Groups orders by supplier automatically  
✅ **Professional reports** - PDF generation for each distributor

- Multi-location businesses needing coordination
- Anyone who wants faster, easier inventory tracking
